## Add the connector

1. In [Claude Desktop](https://claude.ai/download), open **Customize > Connectors**.
2. Click **+ Add**, then **Add custom connector**.
3. Name it `Stophy` and paste this URL, then click **Continue**.

```text
https://mcp.stophy.dev/mcp
```

4. Keep the sign-in settings Claude detects and click **Add**. Claude opens stophy.dev, where you sign in or sign up and approve the connection.

The same connector works in Claude on the web and on mobile. On the Free plan, Claude allows one custom connector.

## Try it

In a new chat, click **+**, then **Connectors**, and turn on Stophy. Then ask this.

```text
Use Stophy to find the top Reddit posts about Bun this week and summarize them.
```

If Stophy doesn't answer, check that the connector is on for this chat. If it asks you to sign in again, you signed out of stophy.dev, which disconnects the app.
