## Options add to the price

Options add to a scrape when they return something. They also apply to crawl, batches and search page reads. [Browser actions](/scrape/browser-actions) and CSS rules add nothing.

| Option | Adds per 1,000 pages |
| - | - |
| `json` with a schema or a prompt, `summary`, `highlights`, `question`, `product`, `menu`, `changeTracking` | **$3** each |
| `checkPromptInjection` | **$2.50**, when the scan runs |
| PDF | **$0.50** of text from its text layer, **$2.50** of text read from its picture. With `maxChars`, `offset` or `query` you pay for the share of pages returned, one page at least. |
| `zeroDataRetention` | **$2** per 1,000 scraped URLs, not per PDF page. Not charged on an error status. See [Zero data retention](/production/zero-data-retention). |

They stack. Scraping 10,000 pages with `json` costs 10,000 × ($1 + $3) ÷ 1,000 = $40.

A scrape costs at most $0.1165 a page.

## When you are charged

| Case | Charge |
| - | - |
| A page reads fine | Full price |
| An answer from a stored copy within `maxAge` | Full price |
| A readable 404 or 410 | Page price waived |
| A page you cannot read | Nothing |
| A call that fails | Nothing |
| An extract that finds no field | Nothing |

[Interact on a scrape](/scrape/interact) costs the [browser session](#browser) price.

## Balance

Top up at [stophy.dev/billing](https://stophy.dev/billing) from $10 to $10,000. All purchases are final.

The first top-up saves your card and moves the account from `free` to `paid`. [Rate limits](/production/rate-limits) step up at $10, $100 and $500 topped up in all.

### Auto top-up

Turn it on at the same page after a top-up. Pick an amount from $10 to $10,000 and a monthly limit of at least that amount.

When a call needs more than your balance, Stophy charges the card and runs the call. At the monthly limit, or if the card declines, the call fails and the message says which.

### When the balance is too low

A call your balance cannot cover returns `402` and charges nothing. Stophy checks the balance against the most the call can cost, so a call can fail while the balance covers the usual price.

The error body has `availableUsd` (your balance), `requiredUsd` (the most the call can cost) and `billingUrl` (where to top up). A browser task you cannot cover tells you to add funds or lower `maxCost`.

## Free

- A call that fails, a page that returns 404 or 410, a page you cannot read, and an extract that finds no field.
- Status, results, cancel and list calls for jobs and monitors, and webhook delivery reads.
- Account calls: [limits, usage, history and logs](/account/usage), `GET /v1/endpoints`, `GET /v1/parsers`, `GET /v1/status`, sign-up and sign-in.
- Creating, listing and reading browser profiles, extensions, network policies, recordings and replays. Only session time and traffic cost.
- [Feedback](/production/agent-feedback). A bad rating refunds that call, up to $0.10 a day.

## Read what a call cost

- The `x-cost-usd` header on every response.
- `data.metadata.costUsd` on scrape and parse. Job status, account, browser and monitor calls put `costUsd` at the top level of the body.
- `GET /v1/limits` shows your balance.
- `GET /v1/logs` shows what each recent call cost. See [Usage and logs](/account/usage).
