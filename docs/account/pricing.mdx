You prepay a dollar balance. Each call takes its price out when it finishes. The catalog in `/openapi.json` lists the same prices: `costUsd` is the least a call can cost and `maxCostUsd` the most.

### Scrape add-ons

They add to the $1 per 1,000 pages when they return something. [Browser actions](/scrape/browser-actions) and CSS rules add nothing.

| Option | Adds per 1,000 pages |
| - | - |
| `json` with a schema or a prompt, `summary`, `highlights`, `question`, `product`, `menu`, `changeTracking` | $3 each |
| `checkPromptInjection` | $2.50 when the scan runs |
| PDF | $0.50 of text from its text layer, $2.50 of text read from its picture. With `maxChars`, `offset` or `query` you pay for the share of pages returned, one page at least. |
| `zeroDataRetention` | $2 per 1,000 scraped URLs, not per PDF page. Not charged on an error status. See [Zero data retention](/production/zero-data-retention). |

An answer from a stored copy within `maxAge` costs a full read. A page you cannot read costs nothing. The page price is waived for a readable 404 or 410.

## Balance

A new account gets $2 once, with no card. Top up at [stophy.dev/billing](https://stophy.dev/billing) from $10 to $10,000. The balance does not expire. All purchases are final.

The first top-up saves your card and moves the account from `free` to `paid`. [Rate limits](/production/rate-limits) step up at $10, $100 and $500 topped up in all.

After a top-up you can turn on auto top-up at the same page. Pick an amount from $10 to $10,000 and a monthly limit of at least that amount. When a call needs more than your balance, Stophy charges the card and runs the call. At the monthly limit, or if the card declines, the call fails with `insufficientCredits` and the message says which.

A call your balance cannot cover returns `402` with `insufficientCredits` and charges nothing. Stophy checks the balance against the most the call can cost, so a call can fail while the balance covers the usual price. `availableUsd` is your balance, `requiredUsd` the most the call can cost, `billingUrl` where to top up. A browser task you cannot cover tells you to add funds or lower `maxCost`.

## Free

* A call that fails, a page that returns 404 or 410, a page you cannot read, and an extract that finds no field.
* Status, results, cancel and list calls for jobs and monitors, and webhook delivery reads.
* Account calls: [limits, usage, history and logs](/account/usage), `GET /openapi.json`, `GET /health`, sign-up and sign-in.
* Creating, listing and reading browser profiles, extensions, network policies, recordings and replays. Only session time and traffic cost.
* [Feedback](/production/agent-feedback). A bad rating refunds that call, up to $0.10 a day.

## Read what a call cost

* `x-cost-usd` header on every response.
* `data.metadata.costUsd` on scrape and parse. Job status, account, browser and monitor calls put `costUsd` at the top level of the body.
* `creditsUsed` and `x-credits-used` are older fields for the same cost in steps of $0.002. Use `costUsd`.
* `GET /v1/limits` shows your balance. `GET /v1/logs` shows what each recent call cost. See [Usage and logs](/account/usage).
