title: "Retention and zero data retention"
description: "See the scope of zero data retention and the limits of published deletion times."
---

The [zero data retention page](/production/zero-data-retention) describes the setting and its feature restrictions. Billing and operational metadata remain, and jobs and browser sessions need temporary state.

For ordinary calls, the application sets a 24-hour lifetime for [stored scrape results](https://github.com/stophydotdev/stophy-api/blob/6b824524/apps/server/src/api/scrapeResult.ts) and [job records](https://github.com/stophydotdev/stophy-api/blob/6b824524/apps/server/src/jobs/jobStore.ts). [Change tracking](https://github.com/stophydotdev/stophy-api/blob/6b824524/apps/server/src/sources/web/scrape/changes.ts) retains a previous snapshot for 30 days. These application limits do not establish physical deletion from every store or backup.

An [API retention audit](https://github.com/stophydotdev/stophy-api/pull/1447) inventories application stores and proposes changes to private processing. The changes have not been merged, so its private-processing behavior is not a statement about the deployed service. The audit distinguishes expiry and read windows from physical deletion. Redis snapshots, database backups, object storage lifecycle rules, platform logs, and third-party retention require separate evidence. A 90-day log lookup window does not establish deletion of the underlying ledger after 90 days.
