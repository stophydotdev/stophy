---
title: "Incident response"
description: "Find the security contact and the steps used to assess and respond to a report."
---

Report vulnerabilities privately to [support@stophy.dev](mailto:support@stophy.dev). The [security policy](https://github.com/stophydotdev/stophy-api/blob/6b824524/SECURITY.md) asks reporters not to disclose details publicly before a fix.

The response plan is to assess the affected services and data, contain the problem, preserve relevant evidence, and restore service. Customer notice and contractual deadlines depend on the applicable agreement and law.
