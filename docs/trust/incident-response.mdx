## Response

1. Record the report, time, affected services, and a lead. Classify severity and assess whether personal data may be affected.
2. Contain the incident, preserve relevant logs and configuration, and record actions and timestamps. Restrict evidence access and avoid collecting unnecessary content.
3. Assess impact and restore service. Revoke compromised credentials and coordinate with affected providers when needed.
4. Notify affected customers without undue delay after confirming a personal data breach. Proposed initial notice target: within 72 hours of Stophy's confirmation, subject to legal review and the signed agreement. Send updates as facts change. Do not wait for a complete investigation when the agreement or law requires earlier notice.
5. Record the cause, impact, timeline, corrective actions, owners, and due dates. Share an appropriate post-mortem with affected customers. Review completion internally.

Report a vulnerability to [support@stophy.dev](mailto:support@stophy.dev), as directed by [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/stophydotdev/stophy-api/blob/6b824524/SECURITY.md). Response coverage, notification deadlines, and evidence retention require approval.
