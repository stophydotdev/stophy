**Draft for review. Not an executed agreement.** A public template with an open licence has not been verified, so this draft is not attributed to one. Select and verify a suitable template and its licence before approval. Legal review, factual checks, and completed schedules are required.

## Parties and scope

Customer: _______________. Stophy legal entity and address: _______________. Effective date: _______________. Principal service agreement: _______________.

The customer is the controller and Stophy is the processor for personal data processed to provide the services, unless the parties agree otherwise. The subject matter, duration, nature, purpose, data categories, and categories of people are in Schedule A. The parties must also describe any processing in which those roles differ.

## Processing instructions and personnel

Stophy will process personal data only on the customer's documented instructions, including instructions about transfers, unless applicable law requires otherwise. If law requires other processing, Stophy will inform the customer first unless the law prohibits notice. Stophy will inform the customer if an instruction appears to infringe applicable data protection law.

Stophy will ensure that people authorized to process personal data are bound by confidentiality or a suitable legal duty of confidentiality. The parties must confirm the actual access controls in Schedule B.

## Security and subprocessors

Stophy will implement the technical and organizational measures agreed in Schedule B, taking account of Article 32. The measures must reflect verified controls. The [data map](/trust/subprocessors) is an incomplete inventory and is not yet an approved subprocessor list.

The customer gives the form of authorization chosen here: specific or general authorization, to be agreed. Before adding or replacing a subprocessor, Stophy will notify the customer using the notice period and objection process agreed here: _______________. Stophy will impose equivalent applicable data protection obligations by contract and remain responsible to the customer for the subprocessor's performance as required by law.

## Assistance and incidents

Taking account of the nature of processing, Stophy will assist the customer through appropriate technical and organizational measures, insofar as possible, to answer requests from data subjects exercising their rights. Stophy will assist with security, breach notification, impact assessments, and prior consultation under Articles 32 to 36, taking account of the nature of processing and information available to Stophy.

Stophy will notify the customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach. The parties will agree contact details, notice contents, and any shorter contractual deadline: _______________. The proposed [incident plan](/trust/incident-response) is not a substitute for a signed agreement.

## Return, deletion, and audit

At the customer's choice, Stophy will delete or return personal data after the services end, and delete existing copies unless applicable law requires retention. The parties must agree the method, timing, backup treatment, and legally required retention in Schedule A. See [retention](/trust/retention) for open verification items.

Stophy will make available information necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 and allow and contribute to audits, including inspections, by the customer or an authorized auditor. The parties must agree reasonable notice, confidentiality, security, scope, frequency, and costs without limiting applicable legal rights.

## International transfers

Locations, transfer roles, and safeguards are to confirm. If a transfer requires a transfer mechanism, the parties will execute the applicable standard contractual clauses and complete their annexes and any required supplementary measures before the transfer. This draft does not itself incorporate completed transfer clauses.
