### `linkedin-jobs`

A LinkedIn jobs search page with keywords, a location or a company filter. Example: `https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/search?keywords=software%20engineer&location=United%20States&f_TPR=r604800`

Returns `results`, `cursor`, `page`, `total`.

Options:
- `company`: One company or up to five: LinkedIn URLs, slugs like google, or company ids.
- `easyApply`: LinkedIn's own Easy Apply.
- `fewApplicants`: LinkedIn's own Under 10 applicants.
- `page`

### `linkedin-job`

A LinkedIn job page or lnkd.in share link. Example: `https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/4476983365`

Returns `id`, `url`, `title`, `company`, `location`, `isRemote`, `salary`, `employmentType`, `publishedAt`, `description`.

### `indeed-search`

An Indeed job search page with a search q or a place l, like indeed.com/jobs?q=nurse&l=Austin,+TX. Example: `https://www.indeed.com/jobs?q=software+engineer&l=Austin%2C+TX`

Returns `results`, `cursor`, `page`, `total`.

Options:
- `country`
- `distanceMiles`: Indeed's own Distance from the location. 0 is the exact location only.
- `datePosted`: Indeed's own Date posted. A relisted job keeps its first publishedAt and shows the new date in indexedAt.
- `remote`: Indeed's own Remote filter: Remote, or Hybrid work.
- `jobType`: Indeed's own Job type.
- `experienceLevel`: Indeed's own Experience level.
- `education`: Indeed's own Education.
- `sort`: Indeed's own Sort by.
- `cursor`

### `upwork-search`

An Upwork job search page, like upwork.com/nx/search/jobs/?q=react. Example: `https://www.upwork.com/nx/search/jobs/?q=react&sort=recency`

Returns `results`, `cursor`, `page`, `total`.

Options:
- `jobType`
- `experienceLevel`
- `workload`: Upwork's hours needed: full time, part time, or as needed. Only hourly jobs say this, so the search stays hourly.
- `duration`: Upwork's project length: under one month, one to three months, three to six months, or more than six months. Only hourly jobs say this, so the search stays hourly.
- `minHourlyRate`: Lowest hourly rate in USD. A job matches when its posted range overlaps this minimum, so 25 to 50 matches a minimum of 50. The search stays hourly.
- `maxHourlyRate`: Highest hourly rate in USD. Send it with minHourlyRate. The job's posted range overlaps both, and the search stays hourly.
- `page`

### `glassdoor-salaries`

A Glassdoor company page (overview, reviews or salaries). Example: `https://www.glassdoor.com/Overview/Working-at-Google-EI_IE9079.11,17.htm`

Returns `jobTitle`, `reports`, `totalPayMin`, `totalPayMedian`, `totalPayMax`, `basePayMin`.
