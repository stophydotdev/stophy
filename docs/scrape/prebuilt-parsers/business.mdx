### `crunchbase-company`

A Crunchbase organization page, like crunchbase.com/organization/stripe. Example: `https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/stripe`

Returns `name`, `type`, `leadInvestors`, `investorCount`.

### `g2-reviews`

A G2 product reviews page, like g2.com/products/slack/reviews. Example: `https://www.g2.com/products/slack/reviews`

Returns `results`, `cursor`, `page`, `total`.
