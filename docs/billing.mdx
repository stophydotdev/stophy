Most calls cost $1.50 per 1,000, $1.20 from $100 and $0.80 from $500. New accounts start with $1.50 free, and a call that fails or finds nothing costs nothing.

## Price

| You top up | Most calls cost | Per 1,000 calls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Less than $100 | $0.0015 | $1.50 |
| $100 or more | $0.0012 | $1.20 |
| $500 or more | $0.0008 | $0.80 |

Top up at [stophy.dev](https://stophy.dev), from $10 to $10,000 at a time. Your balance never expires.

## What a call costs

Of the 137 endpoints, 117 cost $0.0015 and 1 costs $0.003. Fifteen are priced per unit and 4 are free. These prices are for top-ups under $100, and the larger tiers cut each one by the same share.

| Cost | Endpoints |
| --- | --- |
| $0.003 | `zillow.search` |
| $0.0015 per 10 images | `google.images`, `web.images` |
| $0.0015 per 10 articles | `web.news` |
| $0.0015 per 10 listings | `craigslist.search` |
| $0.0015 per 10 apps | `appstore.search`, `appstore.top`, `google.play.search` |
| $0.0015, plus $0.0015 for each result read in full | `google.search`, when `scrapeResults` is on |
| $0.0015 per 10 results, plus $0.0015 for each result read in full | `web.search`, when `scrapeResults` is on |
| $0.0015 a page, $0.003 when a page takes more work to load, $0.0075 on the hardest pages to reach | `web.scrape`, `web.crawl`, `web.crawl.start`, `web.batch` |
| $0.003, or $0.0075 on the hardest pages to reach, for a screenshot, actions or `waitFor` | `web.scrape`, `web.batch` |
| Free | `web.crawl.status`, `web.crawl.cancel`, `web.batch.status`, `web.batch.cancel` |
| $0.0015, plus $0.0015 per 30 seconds of audio | `instagram.transcript` |
| $0.0015 with captions, or $0.003 plus $0.0015 per 10 seconds of audio | `tiktok.transcript` |

Every other endpoint costs $0.0015, however many results come back. Starting a `web.crawl.start` or `web.batch` job is free, and each page is charged when it finishes. A `web.scrape` answer from a stored copy costs $0.0015.

Each endpoint's price is on its page in the [API reference](/api-reference/introduction). The catalog has it too, as `credits` and, when the price varies, `pricing`.

```bash
curl https://api.stophy.dev/v1/endpoints
```

## Transcripts

`youtube.transcript` costs $0.0015. Audio is rounded up to the next 30 seconds for `instagram.transcript` and the next 10 seconds for `tiktok.transcript`. Instagram takes videos up to 5 minutes, which is $0.0165 at most. TikTok takes up to 3 minutes, which is $0.03 at most. A longer video is refused and costs nothing.

When audio was transcribed, the response has `transcribedSeconds`.

## Limit the cost of a long list

`web.search`, `web.news`, `web.images`, `google.images`, `google.ads.advertisers`, `craigslist.search`, `appstore.search`, `appstore.top` and `google.play.search` take `limit`. On the ones priced per 10, a smaller `limit` costs less.

## Pages

One call returns one page. The next page is a new call and costs the same again. See [Page through results](/guides/paging).

## Cost units

Every successful response has `creditsUsed` in the body and an `x-credits-used` header. They count what the call cost in units of $0.0015, so `creditsUsed: 2` is $0.003. Top up $100 or more and each unit costs you $0.0012, or $0.0008 from $500. The catalog's `credits`, the usage totals and the `insufficientCredits` error use the same unit. For your balance and history, use [`GET /v1/usage`](/api-reference/usage) and [`GET /v1/logs`](/api-reference/logs).

## Running out

When your balance is too low, the call answers `402` with code `insufficientCredits` and charges nothing. The error has `requiredCredits` and `availableCredits`, in the same units as `creditsUsed`, and a `billingUrl` to top up.
