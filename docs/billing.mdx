## Credits

Every call is priced in credits, and most calls cost 1 credit. Your account starts with 5,000 free credits, with no card. `creditsUsed` in each response is what the call cost.

## Buy credits

Top up at [stophy.dev](https://stophy.dev/billing), from $10 to $10,000 at a time. Credits cost less once your top-ups reach $100, $500 and $2,000:

| Your top-ups so far | 1,000 credits cost |
| --- | --- |
| Less than $100 | $2.00 |
| $100 or more | $1.50 |
| $500 or more | $1.20 |
| $2,000 or more | $0.90 |

A first $10 top-up buys 5,000 credits. Your credits never expire.

## What a call costs

Each endpoint's [reference page](/api-reference/introduction) shows its price.

| Endpoint | Price |
| --- | --- |
| [Search](/api-reference/endpoint/search) | 2 credits per 10 results from each source, plus 1 credit for each web result that comes back with its page text when `scrapeOptions` is on |
| [Scrape](/api-reference/endpoint/scrape) | 1 credit for any page. `json` with a schema or a prompt, `summary` and `highlights` add 4 credits each when they return something. A PDF adds 1 credit for each page read. Each action adds 2 credits. |
| [Map](/api-reference/endpoint/map) | 1 credit per call |
| [Parse](/api-reference/endpoint/parse) | 1 credit per call |
| [Crawl](/api-reference/endpoint/crawl) | 1 credit a page. Starting the job is free. |
| [Batch scrape](/api-reference/endpoint/batch-scrape) | Each page costs what scrape would. Starting the job is free. |
| [Extract](/api-reference/endpoint/extract) | 5 credits for most pages: the page's own price plus 4 credits. A call that finds none of the fields is free. |
| [Browser task](/api-reference/endpoint/browser-task) | 3 credits for each step the task takes, up to `maxSteps` and `maxCost` |
| Status, cancel, catalog and account calls | Free |

A page that cannot be read costs nothing. A page that comes back with a 404 or 410 status is charged like any page. A scrape answered from a stored copy within `maxAge` is free. Crawl and batch scrape charge each page when it finishes.

### Parsers

Scrape with a [parser](/guides/parsers) costs the parser's price, and most parsers cost 1 credit a call. These cost more, or count what comes back:

| Parser | Price |
| --- | --- |
| `zillow-search` | 2 credits per call |
| `google-maps-reviews` | 1 credit per 10 reviews |
| `appstore-search`, `appstore-top`, `google-play-search` | 1 credit per 10 apps |
| `craigslist-search` | 1 credit per 10 listings |
| `reddit-post` | 1 credit per 200 comments |
| `instagram-transcript` | 1 credit plus 1 credit for every 30 seconds of audio, up to 5 minutes |
| `tiktok-transcript` | 1 credit when the video has captions; otherwise 2 credits plus 1 credit for every 10 seconds of audio, up to 3 minutes |

The [parser list](https://api.stophy.dev/v1/parsers) has every parser's `price`.

## What you were charged

`creditsUsed` in the response, or the `x-credits-used` header, is what the call cost. Scrape puts it in `data.metadata.creditsUsed`. [Limits](/api-reference/endpoint/limits) shows your balance in credits, and [Logs](/api-reference/endpoint/logs) lists recent calls with their cost.

## Running out

A call your balance can't cover answers `402` with code `insufficientCredits` and charges nothing. The `x-required-credits` and `x-available-credits` headers say what the call needs and what you have.
