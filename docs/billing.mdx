## Top-ups

| You top up | Most calls cost | Per 1,000 calls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Less than $100 | $0.0015 | $1.50 |
| $100 or more | $0.0012 | $1.20 |
| $500 or more | $0.0008 | $0.80 |

Top up at [stophy.dev](https://stophy.dev), from $10 to $10,000 at a time. Your balance never expires.

## What a call costs

117 of the 137 endpoints cost $0.0015 a call, however many results come back. A call that fails or finds nothing is free, and every next page is a new call. The rest are below, at the under-$100 price. Larger top-ups cut each one by the same share.

| Cost | Endpoints |
| --- | --- |
| $0.003 | `zillow.search` |
| $0.0015 per 10 images | `google.images`, `web.images` |
| $0.0015 per 10 articles | `web.news` |
| $0.0015 per 10 listings | `craigslist.search` |
| $0.0015 per 10 apps | `appstore.search`, `appstore.top`, `google.play.search` |
| $0.0015, plus $0.0015 for each result read in full | `google.search`, when `scrapeResults` is on |
| $0.0015 per 10 results, plus $0.0015 for each result read in full | `web.search`, when `scrapeResults` is on |
| $0.0015 a page, $0.003 when a page takes more work to load, $0.0075 on the hardest pages to reach | `web.scrape`, `web.crawl`, `web.crawl.start`, `web.batch` |
| $0.003, or $0.0075 on the hardest pages to reach, for a screenshot, actions or `waitFor` | `web.scrape`, `web.batch` |
| Free | `web.crawl.status`, `web.crawl.cancel`, `web.batch.status`, `web.batch.cancel` |
| $0.0015, plus $0.0015 per 30 seconds of audio | `instagram.transcript` |
| $0.0015 with captions, or $0.003 plus $0.0015 per 10 seconds of audio | `tiktok.transcript` |

Starting a `web.crawl.start` or `web.batch` job is free, and each page is charged when it finishes. A `web.scrape` answer from a stored copy costs $0.0015. Each endpoint's reference page and the [catalog](https://api.stophy.dev/v1/endpoints) show its price.

On the endpoints priced per 10, send a smaller `limit` to pay less.

## Transcripts

Audio is rounded up to the next 30 seconds for Instagram and the next 10 seconds for TikTok. Instagram takes videos up to 5 minutes, so at most $0.0165. TikTok takes up to 3 minutes, so at most $0.03. A longer video is refused and costs nothing. The response has `transcribedSeconds` when audio was transcribed.

## Cost units

Every successful response has `creditsUsed` in the body and an `x-credits-used` header. They count what the call cost in units of $0.0015, so `creditsUsed: 2` is $0.003. Top up $100 or more and each unit costs you $0.0012, or $0.0008 from $500. The catalog's `credits`, the [usage](/api-reference/usage) totals and the `insufficientCredits` error use the same unit.

## Running out

A call your balance can't cover answers `402` with code `insufficientCredits` and charges nothing. The error has `requiredCredits`, `availableCredits` and a `billingUrl` to top up.
