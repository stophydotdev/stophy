## Limits

| | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| With a key | 600 requests a minute per account |
| Without a key | 10 requests a minute and 50 a day per IP address |

Without a key you can call [search](/api-reference/endpoint/search), [scrape](/api-reference/endpoint/scrape), [map](/api-reference/endpoint/map) and [parse](/api-reference/endpoint/parse), over HTTP and [MCP](/mcp-server/keyless). They are the endpoints marked keyless on their reference pages. Scrape with a [parser](/guides/parsers) works without a key when the parser is marked `keyless: true` in the [parser list](https://api.stophy.dev/v1/parsers). Paging, crawl, batch scrape, extract and browser tasks need a key. [Sign up](https://stophy.dev/signup) to lift the limits.

Responses include the `X-RateLimit-Limit`, `X-RateLimit-Remaining` and `X-RateLimit-Reset` headers. Without a key they count the day's calls.

## When you go over

| Code | Status | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `rateLimited` | `429` | Wait the number of seconds in `retryAfterSeconds`, which is also the `Retry-After` header, then retry. |
| `freeLimit` | `429` | Get a key at `signupUrl`, or wait for `retryAfterSeconds`. |

[Errors and retries](/guides/errors-and-retries) has a retry loop.
