With a key you can send 600 requests a minute per account. Without one, you get 10 a minute and 50 a day for each IP address.

| | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| With a key | 600 requests a minute per account |
| Without a key | 10 requests a minute and 50 a day per IP address |

Most calls answer within 15 seconds. A few take up to a minute, like reading a page you pick, transcribing audio, or asking ChatGPT or Gemini. Set your client timeout to at least 65 seconds.

## Without a key

Endpoints marked `keyless: true` in the [endpoint catalog](https://api.stophy.dev/v1/endpoints) work with no key, over HTTP and [MCP](/mcp-server/keyless). You get the first page only.

[Sign up](https://stophy.dev/signup) for $1.50 free, the higher limit and every endpoint.

## Headers

Responses include `X-RateLimit-Limit` and `X-RateLimit-Remaining`.

## When you go over

| Code | Status | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `rateLimited` | `429` | You sent more than 600 requests this minute with a key. Wait for the `Retry-After` header, which is also `retryAfterSeconds` in the body, then retry. |
| `freeLimit` | `429` | You used the free calls without a key. Get a key at `signupUrl`, or wait for `retryAfterSeconds`. |

Both are marked `retryable: true`. See [Errors and retries](/guides/errors-and-retries) for a retry loop.
