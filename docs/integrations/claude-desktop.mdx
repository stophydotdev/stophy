Connect [Claude Desktop](https://claude.ai/download) to Stophy to use its tools in your chats. Sign in through the connector rather than entering an API key.

## Add the connector

Add Stophy once, then turn it on for the chats that need it.

1. In Claude Desktop, open **Customize > Connectors**.
2. Click **+ Add**, then **Add custom connector**.
3. Name it `Stophy`, paste `https://mcp.stophy.dev/mcp` and click **Continue**.
4. Keep the sign-in settings Claude detects, click **Continue**, then click **Add**. Claude opens stophy.dev, where you sign in or sign up and approve the connection.

The same connector works in Claude on the web and on mobile. On the Free plan, Claude allows one custom connector.

## Try it

To check the connection, open a new chat and turn on Stophy under **+ > Connectors**. Ask Claude to find the top Reddit posts about Bun this week with Stophy and summarize them.

If Stophy doesn't answer, check that the connector is on for this chat. If it asks you to sign in again, you signed out of stophy.dev, which disconnects the app.
