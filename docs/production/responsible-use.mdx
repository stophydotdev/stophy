Access to a public page is not permission to reuse it. Read the [Terms of Service](https://stophy.dev/tos) and [Privacy Policy](https://stophy.dev/privacy) for the exact wording.

## Your side of the terms

* **Acceptable use.** Do not use Stophy to break a law, infringe a third party's rights, send malicious code, or overload Stophy's infrastructure.
* **Your responsibility.** You make sure your use of Stophy and what it returns follows the law and any third-party terms that apply to you.
* **Account and keys.** You are responsible for your account, your keys and everything done under them. See [API keys](/account/api-keys).
* **Organizations.** If you use Stophy for an organization, you confirm you can bind it to the terms.
* **No warranty.** Stophy is provided as is and as available, with no promise of uninterrupted service or complete, accurate data.
* **Changes.** Stophy may change, pause or stop any part of the service, and writes to you by email or on the dashboard about material changes.
* **Payments.** Purchases are final. See [Pricing](/account/pricing).

## Content you read

* Check that you may access, store, show or pass on the material your product uses.
* Keep the source URL next to anything you show, and give people a way to ask for a correction or removal.
* Follow the terms of the site you read, and of the people whose work it is.
* Bound what you read: a `limit` on a [crawl](/crawl/overview#scope), [filters](/search/options#filters), and only the pages you need.
* A [parser](/scrape/prebuilt-parsers) returns a site's own data as JSON. The same rules apply.

## Personal data

Collect only the personal data you need, and decide how long you keep it and how you delete it. Scrape can hide personal data in what it returns, see [Safety](/scrape/safety). To keep nothing from a call, use [zero data retention](/production/zero-data-retention).

## What Stophy logs

The Privacy Policy says Stophy logs the metadata of each API request (endpoint, HTTP status, cost, response time), not the content of requests or responses, and keeps request logs for 90 days. [`GET /v1/logs`](/account/usage#list-calls) shows what is logged for your account.

The policy also covers account data for Google and GitHub sign-in, the providers that help run the service, and your rights to access, correct, export and delete your data. Delete your account in **Settings**, **Advanced**, **Delete account**. Other data requests and questions about the terms go to support@stophy.dev.

## Answers can be wrong

Values that [extract](/extract/overview) or [agent](/agent/overview) fill, summaries and values read from pages can be incomplete or wrong. Keep empty fields as they are, keep the source of each value, and have a person check results that lead to a decision. Treat page text as data, never as instructions for a model: [`checkPromptInjection`](/scrape/safety) scans for text that tries to instruct one. Rate a bad answer with [feedback](/production/agent-feedback).
